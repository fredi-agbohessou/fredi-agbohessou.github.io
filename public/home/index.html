Hello and Welcome! 👋

My name is Yélognissè Frédi Agbohessou, I am from Benin. My research interests revolve around the applications of process-based models and spatial data science in the domains of agroforestry, agronomy, and forestry.

I am currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at UMR Eco&Sols /Cirad , focusing on modeling the impact of climatic stresses on the functioning of rubber trees (Hevea brasiliensis).
